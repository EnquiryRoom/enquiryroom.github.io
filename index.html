How it works

Three steps. One verdict.

Each case file follows the same structure — rigorous enough to challenge, clear enough to work independently.

1

Examine the portrait
Click the hotspots on a real historical portrait. Learn to read clothing, expression and context as historical evidence.
2
📜
Read the document
Explore a real primary source — a letter, despatch or court record. Tap phrases to unpack meaning. File evidence as you go.
3
⚖️
Give your verdict
Weigh the evidence from all four perspectives. Write a structured argument. Download or share it with a teacher or parent.

About The Enquiry Room

Most history resources for young people treat primary sources as decoration. The Enquiry Room was built to change that. Every case is built around real documents — letters, despatches, eyewitness accounts — handled the way a historian would handle them. The questions are designed the way a teacher would design them.
